Our Farm

Farming is part of our family.

Mimi's Organic Farm is owned and operated by Domitila Tapia and her family. Domitila began working in strawberry fields in Mexico alongside her parents and grandparents before immigrating to California.

After years of working in agriculture, Domitila and her husband decided to build a farm of their own. In 2015, they began growing organically on two acres of land.

Today, Domitila works alongside her husband and daughters, continuing a family tradition of farming while building a healthier future for the next generation.

Farm to School

Growing food for the next generation.

Mimi's Organic Farm works with school districts across California to bring locally grown organic produce into school meal programs. These partnerships create reliable markets for farmers while giving students access to fresh food grown close to home.
